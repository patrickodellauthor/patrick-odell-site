I'm thrilled to finally share this: **Gods and Monsters**, Book One of The Godfire Saga, launches **February 9th, 2027**.

This story has been a long time coming, and I can't wait for you to meet Elias and the gang as they try to figure out what's tearing through Locust Grove, GA.

## What to Expect

Gods and Monsters is character-driven urban fantasy with action, adventure, dark humor, and heart. Expect divine forces, hard choices, and a protagonist who refuses to let a terrible hand define him.

## Buy Links & Events

Preorder links will go live on the [Gods and Monsters book page](/books/gods-and-monsters) and on the [media kit](/media-kit) as retailers confirm availability. I'll also be hosting a launch event — head over to the [events page](/events) for details, and check back as the date gets closer.

More updates, behind-the-scenes notes, and worldbuilding posts are coming as we get closer to launch, and from the bottom of my heart, thank you for being here for this!
